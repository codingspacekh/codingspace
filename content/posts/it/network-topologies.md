A **topology** is the way the devices in a network are connected.

## Star

Every device connects to a central switch.

- A failed cable affects only one device.
- If the switch fails, the whole network fails.

## Bus

All devices share one backbone cable with a terminator at each end.

- Cheap and easy to install.
- Data collisions increase as more devices are added.

## Mesh

Devices connect to several others, so there are many routes.

- Very reliable: traffic is rerouted around failures.
- Expensive in cabling.

> [!EXAM-TIP]
> When asked to *evaluate* a topology, give at least one benefit **and** one drawback.
