## The three laws

1. An object stays at rest or moves at constant velocity unless a resultant force acts on it.
2. The resultant force equals the rate of change of momentum: $F = \dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$, which is $F = ma$ for constant mass.
3. When body A exerts a force on body B, B exerts an equal and opposite force of the same type on A.

> [!WARNING]
> A third-law pair acts on **two different bodies**. Weight and the normal reaction on a book are *not* a third-law pair.

## Worked example

A $2\ \text{kg}$ trolley is pulled with $10\ \text{N}$ against $4\ \text{N}$ of friction. Its acceleration is

$$a = \frac{10 - 4}{2} = 3\ \text{m s}^{-2}.$$
