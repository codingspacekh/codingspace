Ignoring air resistance, a projectile moves with **constant horizontal velocity** and **constant vertical acceleration** $g$.

## Components

Launched at speed $u$ and angle $\theta$:

$$u_x = u\cos\theta, \qquad u_y = u\sin\theta.$$

## Range on level ground

$$R = \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g},$$

which is greatest at $\theta = 45^\circ$.

> [!EXAM-TIP]
> Always state that air resistance is negligible — it is often worth a mark.

## Problems

1. A ball is kicked at $20\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ at $30^\circ$. Find its time of flight ($g = 9.81\ \text{m s}^{-2}$).
2. Show that launch angles $\theta$ and $90^\circ - \theta$ give the same range.
